Clone a Voice

Make a copy of a voice from a short recording. Everything runs on this PC; the clip is never uploaded anywhere.

1. The recording
  • Best: 10–20 seconds of one person speaking clearly. 3 seconds is the minimum, 60 the limit.
  • Only that person: no music, no other voices, no echo or background noise.
  • Natural, continuous speech in full sentences, not single words and not singing.
